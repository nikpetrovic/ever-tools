Your local SMTP inbox for testing email flows — no accounts, no cloud, no rate limits. Ever.

  • Four views HTML, plain text, raw source, and parsed headers side by side
  • Instant search Filter across sender, recipient, and subject in real time
  • Delete & Trash Soft-delete with 15-day auto-purge, multi-select supported
  • 5 accent themes Orange, blue, green, purple, and red — choose what you like
  • System tray Runs quietly in the background, always reachable
